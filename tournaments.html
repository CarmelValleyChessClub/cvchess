G/30 Time Control

Each player gets 30 minutes for the whole game. Fast enough to play three rounds before lunch, slow enough to think about your moves.

Before You Register

Two Things You Need to Know

I run tight tournaments. The pairings depend on every registered player showing up on time, paid up, and ready to play. Read these before you sign up.

💰

Cancellation & Payment Policy

All sales are final. No refunds. Not for illness, not for emergencies, not for any reason. Quad pairings depend on every registered player showing up — pulling someone out after registration creates problems for everyone else in the section.

Payment is due the same day you register. If we don't receive payment within 24 hours, your spot opens back up to the next player on the waitlist.

If you can't make it — you can transfer your spot to another player at a comparable rating. Email me at chessclubcv@gmail.com at least 48 hours before the tournament so I can update the pairings.

⏰

Don't Be Late

Doors open at 9:30am. Be at your board by 9:50am. Round 1 starts at 10:00am sharp.

If you're late: your clock is already running. Every minute you're not at the board comes off your 30.

If you're more than 15 minutes late: you forfeit the round. You can still play the rest of the day, but you'll start at 0–1.

I don't pause rounds, re-pair, or wait. The schedule has zero slack — three rounds, lunch, and a master lecture all in five hours. If you're running late, text (831) 392-7456 so I know.
