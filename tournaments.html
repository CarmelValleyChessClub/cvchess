Tournament Format

What to Expect

⏱️

G/30+5 Time Control

Each player gets 30 minutes for the entire game plus a 5-second increment per move. Fast enough to play 5 rounds in one day, slow enough to think.

♟️

5-Round Swiss

Every player plays all 5 rounds. No elimination. Paired by performance so you face opponents of similar strength as the tournament progresses.

🏆

Two Sections

Players split into two sections by rating. Unrated players start in the lower section — the perfect place for your first tournament experience.

📊

USCF Dual Rated

Games count toward both your Quick rating and Regular rating. Two ratings updated in one tournament.

🆕

First Tournament?

Email Coach JB before the event with any questions. He will make sure you know exactly what to expect before you walk in the door.

📍

Right Here in Carmel Valley
