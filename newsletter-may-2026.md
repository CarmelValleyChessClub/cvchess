## Notes for setup in Mailchimp

1. **Personalization tag:** the `*|FNAME|*` at the top will auto-fill first names. For subscribers with no first name on file, Mailchimp falls back to nothing — so the line reads "Hey ," which looks off. Set a default like "Hey friend," in the merge tag settings (Audience → All contacts → click the FNAME field → set default).

2. **Image suggestion (optional):** in the section about WCM Clara Peña, consider adding her photo (the one from the Venezuelan federation graphic — save as `wcm-clara-pena.jpg` and upload to Mailchimp's content studio). It'll make the section visually stand out.

3. **Section headers:** the 🍣 / 🏆 / ♟️ emoji headers should render well in most email clients but always send yourself a test first.

4. **Link tracking:** Mailchimp tracks link clicks by default. The blog post link, Sushi Slam link, and Summer Camp link are the three to watch.

5. **A/B test idea (optional):** test subject lines #1 ("I don't teach openings") vs #2 ("The kid who didn't know how the pieces moved a month ago") on a 20% split, send winner to the rest 4 hours later.
