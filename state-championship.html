What It Is

Three Days of Prep. One Big Stage.

A focused, three-day tournament-readiness training with Coach JB that ends with your child competing on the biggest scholastic stage in Northern California — the CalChess Super State Championship. Pick the training window that works for your family; both lead to the same March 6 championship.

Option 1 · Winter Session

December 28–30, 2026

9:00 – 10:30 AM daily
Carmel Valley Chess Club · 9 Del Fino Place, Suite 201

Early-bird & refund deadline: December 14, 2026

Option 2 · Late-Winter Session

February 15–17, 2027

9:00 – 10:30 AM daily
Carmel Valley Chess Club · 9 Del Fino Place, Suite 201

Early-bird & refund deadline: February 1, 2027

Both windows run 9:00–10:30 AM daily at the club and prepare your child for the same championship on Saturday, March 6, 2027. Open to any child with basic chess knowledge — no league membership required.

What's Included

$245, All-Inclusive.

State Championship Entry

Boards, pieces, and clocks — all provided by Coach JB. Just bring your child, ready to play.
